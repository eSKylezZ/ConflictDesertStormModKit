                New level
                
    
                An empty level with a sculptable ground and the squad. The sky, fog, sun, sounds and textures come
      from the level you pick (the player's own copy - nothing is shipped). It is saved as a mission straight away;
      shape the ground with Terrain…, add props from the Library, write the script with Script….
                
    
    
    
    
    
                
    
                
  
                
                

  
                
    
                Save as new mission
                
    
                Writes a playable mission into Mods\<mod>: this scene under the new name, its AI
      routes and navigation files, models placed from other levels, and a Lua script that sets the map up (generated
      once - your edits to it are kept). Play it from SINGLE PLAYER › CUSTOM CAMPAIGNS.
